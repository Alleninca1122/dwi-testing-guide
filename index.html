04

Stage 01 Loading — What to Expect

Your submissions are real. This demo is connected to the live database. Every load you submit creates real records and passes that data forward into Stage 02 / 03 / 04 of the pipeline — those stages are still in development and not open for testing yet. Please don't worry about "breaking" anything. If you see a Rack # greyed out and not selectable, that means someone has already tested with it.
Using your new PIN for Employee Sign-off. On your first login with the default PIN 1234, the system requires you to create a new PIN. Later, when submitting your form under "Employee Sign-off" to verify your identity and confirm responsibility for the load, enter your Employee ID and your newly created PIN—not the original 1234.
Not everything is wired up yet. A few things in the Stage 01 form are placeholders for now — please don't be alarmed if they don't do what you'd expect:
  • Photo buttons ("Snap / Upload Photo", "Scan via Phone") in Steps 1–2 — tapping them won't actually save or upload a photo yet.
  • The 5-Step Workflow guidance panel (PPE, weigh-in, venting, rigging, pose, handoff) is a reference checklist to read and follow physically — it isn't tied to any checkbox or field, so it won't block submission on its own.
Two kinds of safety alerts — know the difference:
  • Hard blocks (red, submit disabled): total rack load over 8,000 lb, or an enclosed cavity with no venting and not drilled on site. These can't be overridden — the load must be corrected before you can submit.
  • Warnings (pop up on submit): things like an undersized wire/chain/shackle for the weight entered. You'll see a confirmation pop-up listing them — you can review and choose to continue anyway, the same as you would on paper today.
Please try triggering both at least once during testing (e.g. overload a rack past 8,000 lb, and separately pick a wire count you know is too thin for the weight) so we know both are working as intended.
05

Suggested Things to Try

No fixed script — please explore freely. A few scenarios that would help us the most if you get to them:
  • A normal load: pick a numbered Rack #, add one Job with one or two workpiece lines, and sign off.
  • A "No Rack" (crane direct) load — notice the Rack Fixture options disappear.
  • A numbered rack with the Hook Rack fixture, and separately a workpiece with Railing Comb Rack checked.
  • A workpiece where the pieces aren't all the same weight (uncheck "uniform weight") and use the weight bracket selector instead of typing a number.
  • More than one Job on the same Load, and more than one workpiece line inside a Job.
  • Add an Assistant Operator at sign-off, in addition to the mandatory Primary Operator.
  • Enter Min/Max Oil-Paint or Rust levels far apart, to see the mixed-corrosion warning.
  • After submitting a load, come back and try the Force Release flow on that same Rack #.
