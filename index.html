02

RBAC & Edit Permission Tiers

Audit-Ready
Operator 48h Window
Can modify own submissions within 48 hours; automatically locked afterward to ensure timely shop-floor correction.
Supervisor 2-Week Window (336h)
Can perform managerial overrides, elevated corrections, and shift log sign-offs across any entry.
Admin 10-Week Window (1680h)
Holds full authority for historical data inspection, audit exports, and system-wide parameter configuration.
🔒 Immutable Incremental Audit Logging
All record modifications are stored as incremental appends. The original record remains visible with a red strikethrough, followed by the revised entry displaying the change reason and editor's Employee ID, fully compliant with ISO quality management audit standards.

Devices for Optimal Testing Experience

This DWI system is standardly engineered for 15.6″–21.5″ Industrial Touch PCs (1080p) and 10.5″–12.9″ Rugged Tablets/iPads.

For the best evaluation experience, testing on desktop PCs, laptops, tablets, or iPads is highly recommended. The interface automatically adapts with touch-friendly controls and crisp SVG engineering diagrams.

* Note: While fully functional on smartphones, the testing experience may be less optimal due to compact screen size limitations.
